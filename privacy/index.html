Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

clipper is a personal tool that uploads videos to accounts owned by its operator. This policy explains what it accesses and how that information is handled.

What clipper accesses

  • YouTube (Google): permission to upload videos to the connected channel (youtube.upload).
  • TikTok: basic account information (display name and ID) and permission to upload and publish videos to the connected account.

clipper does not read, collect or store viewers' data, comments, messages, contacts or any information about people other than the account owner.

How it is stored

The sign-in tokens issued by Google and TikTok are stored only on the operator's own computer. They are used solely to upload the operator's videos. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Third-party services

clipper uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. TikTok's handling of data is described in the TikTok Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time from Google's security settings or from TikTok under Settings → Security → Manage app permissions. Stored tokens are deleted when access is revoked or on request.
